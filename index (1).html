    We are building an Australian aerospace and defence company, starting with an aircraft that navigates without help from outside.

    [THESIS-01]

    Why this, and why here.

    Australia is funding sovereign defence capability, and much of it has to be built locally. The gap we are going after is aircraft that keep working when satellite navigation does not.

    Our first aircraft navigates by the landscape beneath it: a camera pointed at the ground, and software that matches what it sees against the surrounding terrain. The same approach carries into the air and space programs behind it.
