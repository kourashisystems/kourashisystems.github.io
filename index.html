Home/Our Mission

Our Mission

To build aircraft, vessels, vehicles and satellites that keep working when the systems around them do not.

[MISSION-01]

Capability that does not depend on someone else's infrastructure.

Modern navigation, communication and positioning all lean on infrastructure that can be jammed, spoofed, or simply switched off. We build systems across air, sea, ground and space that are designed from the outset to keep working when that infrastructure is denied.

That starts with an aircraft that finds its way by the landscape beneath it rather than a satellite signal. The same principle — sense the world directly, decide locally, keep moving — carries into every program that follows it.

  • [01]

    Built in Australia.

    Sovereign capability, designed and built by an Australian team for Australian conditions.

  • [02]

    Independent by design.

    Every program is built to keep operating when the systems around it are degraded or unavailable.

  • [03]

    One approach, four domains.

    Air, sea, ground and space programs share the same navigation and sensing foundation.

Investor Relations

We are building an Australian aerospace and defence company across air, sea, ground and space, starting with an aircraft that navigates without help from outside.

[THESIS-01]

Why this, and why here.

Australia is funding sovereign defence capability, and much of it has to be built locally. The gap we are going after is aircraft that keep working when satellite navigation does not.

Our first aircraft navigates by the landscape beneath it: a camera pointed at the ground, and software that matches what it sees against the surrounding terrain. The same approach carries into the sea, ground and space programs behind it.
